Many scientific problems already come with a physical model that is too slow, or not differentiable, to use inside fitting or optimization. Here I make the physics differentiable, or replace its expensive parts with neural surrogates, so that gradients can be used end to end. Examples are wind farm layout and calibration {% cite bankestad2025diffwake %}, exploring which nanoparticle structures fit a scattering measurement {% cite bankestad2026saxs %}, and fast surrogates for fluid simulation {% cite bankestad2024flexible %}. The CoSiMa project carries the scattering work over to soft materials.
