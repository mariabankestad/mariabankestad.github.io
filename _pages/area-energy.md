Energy systems and fluid flows are governed by physics we understand well but that is expensive to simulate and hard to optimize over. Here that means a differentiable wind farm solver for layout optimization and calibration against operational data {% cite bankestad2025diffwake %}, and equivariant graph networks as fast surrogates for fluid simulation {% cite bankestad2024flexible %}.
