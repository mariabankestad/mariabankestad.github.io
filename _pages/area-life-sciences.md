Biological structure is three-dimensional and usually measured only indirectly. Two projects bring geometry-aware and differentiable methods to it: modelling how enzymes interact with cellulose, and exploring which internal structures of drug-delivery lipid nanoparticles are compatible with an X-ray scattering measurement {% cite bankestad2026saxs %}.
