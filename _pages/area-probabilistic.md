When measurements are expensive, a model has to say how certain it is and where to measure next. This theme is about Gaussian and elliptical processes and the experimental-design methods built on them. A thread through the recent work is the boundary bias of Gaussian processes: where it comes from {% cite bankestad2026boundary %}, how to correct it while adapting to what is measured {% cite jarl2026observation %}, and how it shapes exploration in Bayesian optimization {% cite bankestad2026hidden %}. Elliptical processes extend the model family beyond Gaussian noise {% cite bankestad2023variational %}.
