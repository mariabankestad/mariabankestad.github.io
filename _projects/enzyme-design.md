Cellulose is the most abundant polymer on Earth, and enzymes can modify it in different ways. The aim of this project is to find, and eventually generate, enzymes that interact with cellulose and change its properties in a chosen way.

We have built a large database of carbohydrate-active enzymes, and I am developing equivariant models of how enzymes interact with cellulose. An interaction between an enzyme and a carbohydrate is a relationship between two 3D structures, which is where models that respect the symmetries of the geometry are a natural fit.

The longer-term aim is to use these models for generating enzymes and selecting which candidates to test experimentally. This is ongoing work.
